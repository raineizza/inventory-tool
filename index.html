    How to use

    For warehouse staff counting newly arrived stock.

    1. 1

      Open Start counting

      From the home screen. The top bar shows whether the scanner is connected to the website (green) or in demo mode (orange).

    2. 2

      Scan each item

      Camera: press Start camera, hold the item's code in the frame until it beeps, then take it away.
      USB scanner: just scan. The counting screen catches it wherever you clicked last.

    3. 3

      Check the list

      Every scan adds 1 to that product. Fix mistakes with − and +, or remove a product with the bin. Not recognised means the website doesn't know that code: remove it, or check the product on the website.

    4. 4

      Press OK

      Check the summary, then press Confirm. The units are added to the website's stock and the screen clears for the next product.

    5. 5

      Start over if you need to

      Recount clears everything on the screen. Nothing is sent.

    If something goes wrong

    • "Website not connected" in the top bar: click it to open Settings, then press Test connection.
    • No answer from the website after OK: press OK again without changing the count. It won't be added twice.
    • The camera won't start: allow camera access in the address bar, and close other apps that use the camera.
    • Closed the page by accident? The count is kept. Open the scanner again and press Continue counting.

    For admins

    To connect a website, install its stock-api folder, open stock-api/setup.php on that website, then copy the API address and token into Settings.
